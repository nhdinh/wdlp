## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Mutable manifest -> reviewer signature | Editable provenance and hashes are untrusted until a reviewer-controlled key signs the exact canonical body. |
| Signer certificate -> trusted reviewer policy | Chain validity alone is insufficient; identity, machine/domain role, procedure, EKU, validity, and timestamp policy must match external trust inputs. |
| Human display -> published attestation | Publication must correspond to the complete payload affirmatively reviewed. |
| Concurrent processes -> canonical manifest | Multiple publishers must serialize or fail with an explicit conflict without lost updates. |
