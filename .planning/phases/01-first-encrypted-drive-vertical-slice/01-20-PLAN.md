## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| WinFsp callbacks → authenticated store | Untrusted names, buffers, offsets, flags, concurrency, and cancellation must preserve durable and integrity semantics. |
| Corrupt/incomplete backing records → recovery | Authentication must precede plaintext release or generation selection. |
| Restart/reboot → key/config/store remount | Recovery ordering must prevent stale, corrupt, or unauthenticated state from mounting. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01-20-01 | Tampering/Information disclosure | corruption mapping | high | mitigate | Separate content/metadata corruption, authenticate before copy, exact integrity status, encrypted evidence preservation, and zero-plaintext checks. |
| T-01-20-02 | Tampering/Denial of service | disk-full publication | high | mitigate | Injected NoSpace before pointer publication, exact disk-full status, baseline-hash readback, and no mixed generation. |
| T-01-20-03 | Tampering | restart/reboot recovery | high | mitigate | Authenticate credential, current/LKG, selected pointer, manifest, and chunks before remount. |
| T-01-20-04 | Tampering | WinFsp runtime provenance | high | mitigate | Prior package approval, pinned installer hash, Authenticode verification, LAB-CLIENT01 guard, and delay-load helper only. |
| T-01-20-05 | Elevation/Repudiation | privileged runtime/evidence | high | mitigate | Separate digest-bound 01-20 manifest, baseline/apply/verify/remove/cleanup, role guard, and Plan 01-17 evidence gates. |
